# Ports

Every mod that porter can port today. To get one: open its page (the "How to run" column), download the files it
lists from their official pages, run its one command (`run.bat` on Windows, `run.sh` on Linux), and put the finished
jar into your `mods` folder. Step by step: [Use a ready port](../docs/guides/use-a-ready-port.md).

Your mod is not listed? [Port your own mod](../docs/guides/port-your-own-mod.md), then
[share it](../docs/guides/share-your-port.md).

**Why there are no jars to download.** We do not publish the finished jars of ported mods: the code inside belongs to
the original authors, and redistributing it would break their copyright. Instead each port ships a recipe; you
download the original mod yourself from its official page, and porter builds the port on your computer, for your own
use. Mods with an open licence (such as Torchmaster, MIT) could legally be shared, but we keep one rule for every port.

Columns: **Mode** `full` (the whole mod is ported) or `delta` (an addon to the mod's own older version, which you also
need). **Status** `closure` (the work is measured), `compiles`, `builds`, `scenarios` (its scenario tests pass),
`playable` (tested in a real game). **Scenarios**: share of the port's scenario tests that pass. **Jar may be shared**:
whether the mod's licence (or its author's permission) would allow sharing the jar you build; when it says "no", keep
the jar to yourself.
