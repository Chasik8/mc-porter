# Ports

Ready-made port profiles. Pick a mod, open its folder, follow its README: download the listed files, run one command
(`run.bat` on Windows, `run.sh` elsewhere), put the finished jar into your `mods` folder.

New here? [Use a ready port](../docs/guides/use-a-ready-port.md). Your mod is not listed?
[Port your own mod](../docs/guides/port-your-own-mod.md), then [share it](../docs/guides/share-your-port.md).

Status: `closure` (planned work measured), `compiles`, `builds`, `scenarios` (scenario tests pass on both versions),
`playable` (tested in a real game). "Jar may be shared" says whether the ported jar may be published (open licence or the
author's permission); when it is "no" everybody builds it from their own copy of the original.
